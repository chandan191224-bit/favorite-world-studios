# Visual review — exact-query sources 01–10

Reviewed 2026-10-30. Selection comes from `sources.json`, searched with **timeline animation after effects**. All ten local contact sheets were opened with `view_image` and visually inspected. Evidence is the downloaded opening sample, **not the entire tutorial**. Sheets have approximately one selected frame every three seconds (labels give approximate source time). Motion descriptions describe changes between these frames; precise easing curves and frame timing were not measured. Source 09 lasts roughly six seconds, so only two frames exist. No private/removed source occurs in this group.

The key correction to the first studio concept is visible in these references: most documentary examples use an extended scene that passes outside the viewport. A close view visits dated images or cutouts, pauses, then travels or pulls back. A fit-all infographic is useful as an overview shot, but does not reproduce those sequences by itself. The sources are visual design references; their historical captions and political claims were not fact-checked or adopted as studio content.

## 01 — Flat Pack FX: Stylized Timeline Animation

- [Creator video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9AtjrBHF560) · Evidence: `9AtjrBHF560/contact.jpg` · sampled 0–36s.
- **Observed:** At about 4.5s a bright lime horizontal rail crosses a black field. A white ring anchors an upright right-angle connector to an upper illustration and bold date. At about 7.5s the first illustration has travelled toward the left edge while a second lower illustration and date occupy the right half. The route continues beyond the current frame.
- **Geometry/media:** Thin white callout elbows; luminous ring markers; alternating upper/lower monochrome architectural cutouts rather than equal-height text cards.
- **Motion/use:** Draw-on rail, connector/media reveal and lateral passage between historical beats. Useful when narration names successive dated events.
- **Limit:** The sampled demo is brief; after about 10s the sample mainly shows the presenter and composition setup. Full construction and exact glow settings were not reviewed.

## Implementation implications from these ten inspected samples

1. Keep scene/world coordinates independent from export viewport. Allow offscreen events and rail extensions; provide editable close visit, hold, travel and pullback camera segments.
2. Treat year/date overlays as a separate family: horizontal date slider, vertically rolling year stack and bottom-footage ruler are distinct from media chronologies.
3. Support photographs, alpha cutouts, framed archive images and alternate upper/lower placements. Large current media should be readable before showing the complete chronology.
4. Allow real per-event hold and arrival timing; the 3-second contact-sheet spacing cannot justify copying numeric easing or reveal timings from the references.
5. Colour/texture are finishes, not separate geometry counts. The 36s samples often contain sponsor promos, presenter shots and editor UI; exclude those from effect counts.
