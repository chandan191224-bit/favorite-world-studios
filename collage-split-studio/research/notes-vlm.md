# Research notes — vision-model review of reference images (2026-11 sandbox pass)

11 images found via web image search were reviewed with a vision model (queries: Johnny Harris collage video frame,
scrapbook stop-motion collage, split-screen VS badge, film strip / contact sheet). Useful, concrete observations that
shaped the layouts:

**Scrapbook / junk-journal look (strongest hits):**
- Backgrounds: white painted wood, cream dot-grid notebook, kraft paper.
- Photos flush-mounted with thin white/pink borders; instax prints with a **thick white bottom band**; one print
  standing upright with a soft shadow.
- Hand-torn kraft panels as backing scraps; stickers with **thin white sticker outlines**; letter-stamp labels.
- Handwritten cursive labels in dark ink on white cards and die-cut note shapes; white gel pen on dark photo borders.
- Soft one-directional (window-light) shadows; whole spreads slightly curved, elements mostly un-rotated in real
  craft scrapbooks — the video look adds tilt/jitter for the "hand-placed" feel.

**35 mm contact sheet (exactly as in `contact-sheet-develop`):**
- Six horizontal strips of 6 frames on a **black** background, thin black gaps between frames.
- Small rounded-rect **sprocket holes along both edges** of each strip.
- **Frame numbers 1–36 under the frames**, plus small frame codes ("1A", "2A").
- Repeated white edge text per strip (film brand + emulsion) — we use generic text ("COLLAGE 400 · 36 EXP").

**Film strip template:**
- Rounded-rect sprocket holes, orange/yellow edge markings, frame counters with little arrows.

**Split-screen / VS:** web results were mostly off-target (product photos, game art). The look we implement follows the
well-known CapCut/ Shorts convention: vertical 50/50 or diagonal divider, thick white line, round VS badge in a bold
accent colour, name labels at the top of each half (or under cut-outs), both halves slowly zoom in.

No real brand names, logos or people are reproduced anywhere in the studio (user rule). Raw search hits:
`image-search-results.json`.
