Your first investigation

  1. Pick a template or start a blank board.
  2. Drag clues. Double-click one to edit its text. Add your photos and videos from Elements.
  3. Select a clue, choose String, then click another clue.
  4. Choose a camera preset, then customize each shot in the Camera inspector.
  5. Play to preview the animation. Save the editable project and export a silent video.

Space: play/pause · Ctrl+S: save · Ctrl+Z: undo · Ctrl+D: duplicate · Delete: remove · Arrow keys: move selected clue (Shift: larger step). On phone: drag clues; pinch to zoom; two fingers to pan.
