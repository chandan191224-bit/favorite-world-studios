# Film Look Studio — catalogue: light leaks, film burns, old-film texture

Source: 42 contact sheets (15 YouTube refs), frames sampled every 0.25–2.2 s (timestamps in the
sheet labels). Sheet frames are 200 px wide, so all sizes are % of frame width (W) / height (H)
and colours are hex estimates from the thumbnails (±10%). "Sample" = one sheet frame.
Where a value is inferred, not read off the screen, it is marked *(inferred)*.

## Part 2 — merged catalogue by family (refs = number of the 15 videos showing it)

### A. Light leaks (refs 6: WRgB, b-jt, oNLb, vxDC, pIr64, CD49 partly)
Blend: **Screen or Add** on footage (all packs ship on black; 3 tutorials say Screen/Add); opacity
40–100%. Render as 2–4 soft radial/elliptical blobs + one linear gradient, coloured by a tritone ramp.
1. **Warm pulse leak** (5 refs) — palette ramp black → `#8B1A0A` → `#E86A1E` → `#F5A623` →
   `#FFF4C8` → white. Blob diameter 40–100% H, feather = radius (Gaussian). Anchored at an edge or
   corner (top-right most common, then left/bottom-left). Envelope: attack 0.7–1.5 s, hold 0.3–0.8 s,
   release 1–2 s, ember tail 1–3 s at 5–10% brightness. Drift 5–15% W/s.
2. **Drifting ember blobs** (2 refs) — 2–4 blobs 25–50% H, amber core/red rim, Perlin-driven
   brightness 0.2–1.0 over 6–9 s, drift 5–10% W/s. Loopable ambient leak.
3. **Edge flood** (2 refs) — one side floods red-orange `#D04020`; gradient from edge covers 30–60% W;
   2–4 s.
4. **Cool / prismatic leaks** (3 refs) — ice blue `#3A5280`→`#C8D8F0` smoky wisps; violet `#6A30E0` →
   cyan `#40E0D0`; magenta `#C030C0`; deep blue `#1020A0` vignette leak; teal `#2E6A5E`. Same envelopes,
   often shorter (0.5–1 s).
5. **Smoke/wisp texture** (1 ref, b-jt) — leak luminance modulated by stretched fractal noise
   (scale 30–60% W, stretched 2:1 diagonally) for streaky "smoke".
6. **Procedural leak recipe** (Texturelabs): fractal noise, large scale, blurred → CC Toner tritone →
   screen; evolution very slow (≈0.1 cycle/s). Use 16-bit/float to avoid banding (add 1/255 dither).

### B. Film burns (refs 5: CD49, 22vk, dMIn, ZedI, b-jt)
Blend: Add/Screen (CD49, dMIn), whiteout at the cut.
1. **Classic burn transition** (CD49 ×10, dMIn) — 4 phases: dark-red tint rise 0.9 s → full-frame
   red wash `#B0301E` 2–3 s with a moving dark vignette blob → **hot sweep**: gradient white `#FFFBEF` /
   yellow `#F6C030` / orange `#E8701C` / red `#C0301C` / dark `#3A0C06` laid along X, travelling
   20–40% W per second, white covers 30–60% W at peak (0.5–0.9 s; cut here) → brown `#2A0E08` tail
   1–3 s. Total 5–10 s; short variant 1.5–2 s (rise 0.4, white 0.5, decay 0.8).
2. **Burn-hole / melt edge** (1 ref) — irregular noise-displaced boundary, bright rim 1% H
   `#FFE8A0`, black interior, expands ~10% H per frame.
3. **Film-edge burn** (2 refs, dMIn, b-jt) — vertical band 8–12% W at one side (orange `#F08A30` or
   dark), with ghost edge-code letters and sprocket holes; rotate 90° for horizontal burns.
4. **Over-exposed clear film** (1 ref, ZedI) — radial gradient centre `#FFF2E8` → edge `#C0303A`,
   burnt corners `#3A1410`.

### C. Flares / flashes / whiteouts (refs 7: WRgB, b-jt, oNLb, vxDC, CD49, 22vk, dMIn)
1. **Whiteout at cut** — frame goes to cream `#FFF8EE`/`#FFF6D0` for 0.25–0.8 s; the edit cut is
   placed at the brightest frame. Total transition 1–1.5 s (attack 0.5 s, release 0.4–0.6 s).
2. **Splice / run-out flash** (22vk) — 1–3 frame (40–125 ms) white flashes alternating with black,
   intervals 0.25–1 s, white frames carry dust + hair; projector oval vignette on dim frames.
3. **Hard sliding edge** (22vk, CD49) — a straight black/white boundary sweeps across in 0.25–0.5 s with
   a cyan `#3080C0` (or red) fringe 1% W — frame-line slip.
4. **Bright spot flare** (vxDC, b-jt) — single bright soft spot 10–15% W at an edge, 0.5–1 s.

### D. Grain (refs 4: 9PSP, pIr64, sjgc, og3E)
- Per-frame random luminance noise, size 1–2 px at 1080 (≈0.05–0.1% W), slightly soft (blur 0.5 px),
  monochrome; amount 3–8% (sigma) for "subtle", 10–15% for heavy B&W. Overlay/soft-light blend, more
  visible in mids than in deep blacks/whites. New pattern every frame at 24 fps (hold 2 frames for an
  8 mm feel at 12 fps). Fractal-noise source (9PSP, pIr64): scale ~2–4 px, contrast 150–300%.

### E. Dust & hair (refs 6: 9PSP, pIr64, V8kx, s4Vq, 22vk, og3E)
- **Specks**: 0–8 per frame; size 0.1–1% W (mostly <0.3%); shape round/irregular blobs; **1 frame
  life** (occasionally 2); dark specks (multiply, `#000` at 60–90%) on positives (V8kx, s4Vq);
  white specks (screen) on negative-dirt style (9PSP). Rate: 1 per 8–10 frames for "clean 8 mm",
  3–8 per frame for "damaged".
- **Hair/fibre**: thin curved line 0.15–0.3% W thick, 5–15% W long, dark, 1–4 frames, rare
  (1 per 3–6 s), often near edges.
- **Blobs/stains** (pIr64): soft dark round stains 5–15% H, opacity 10–30%, persist 0.5–2 s.

### F. Scratches (refs 4: b-jt, sjgc, pIr64, 22vk)
- **Vertical line scratches**: 1–3 lines, width 0.25–0.5% W, full height, white (emulsion, screen) or
  dark (base, multiply); x position wanders slowly (±1% W over ~1 s) and lines pop in/out every
  0.5–2 s; opacity flickers 30–80% per frame. Placed 30–70% W typically.
- **Diagonal hairline scratch** (22vk): faint, 1 frame.

### G. Gate / frame / sprockets (refs 6: V8kx, s4Vq, sjgc, og3E, b-jt, ZedI)
- **8 mm projected gate** (s4Vq): window 67% W × 89% H centred; corner radius 4% W; edge feather
  ~0.8% W, edge waviness ±0.3% W (low-freq noise); window tint `#DCD8D4` with hotspot +8% at centre;
  surround `#141410` with halo `#2A2A22` 3% W. Multiply over footage (footage scaled to fill window).
- **Super-8 with sprocket** (V8kx): window x 17→88% W, y 2→95% H, radius 3% W; one sprocket hole
  12.5% W × 28% H at x 4.5–17%, vertically centred, outline `#7A5A48`; base `#08080F`.
- **35 mm edge** (sjgc, b-jt): left margin ~15% W with vertical edge print (e.g. "KODAK 400VC"
  style, amber `#E0A040`, height 3% W, rotated 90°) and sprocket holes ~5% W × 12% H at pitch ~27% H
  (4 perfs per frame); window corner radius ~4% W, burnt dark edge 1–2% W.
- **Custom gate matte** (og3E): rounded rect 93.8% W × 75.9% H, roughened edge (fractal displace
  ~0.4% W, boiling at 2 Hz), inner broken line inset 3%, Gaussian feather 2–3% W, outside = black.
- **Cue marks** (ZedI): black disc 5–7% H at top-right (~85% W, 12% H), 4–8 frames (or 1.5 s held).
- **Projector oval vignette** (22vk): elliptical falloff to black at corners.

### H. Weave / jitter / flicker (refs 5: pIr64, og3E, 22vk, V8kx, s4Vq)
- **Gate weave**: whole picture offset, smooth noise, horizontal ±0.2–0.5% W, vertical ±0.3–0.8% H,
  ~1–3 Hz (og3E wiggle(2,…) = 2 Hz); rotation ±0.1° optional. Frame window moves *less* than picture
  (gate fixed, film moves) — in the overlays the gate itself jitters ≤0.5%.
- **Exposure flicker**: per-frame brightness multiplier, Gaussian random (og3E gaussRandom(1.2,1.8)
  ⇒ normalise to ×0.94–1.06 around 1.0 for subtle, ×0.85–1.15 for heavy), new value every 1–2 frames.
- **Edge boil**: gate-edge roughness re-randomised at 2 Hz (og3E), or every frame at 12 fps (ZedI).
- **Frame-rate stutter** *(inferred from ZedI 12 fps boil)*: hold each texture frame 2 frames at 24 fps.

### I. Halation / bloom (refs 3: wi5y, pIr64 glow, sjgc softness)
- **Halation**: threshold luminance > ~0.8, blur radius 0.5–1.5% W, tint `#FF5030` (red-orange),
  Screen/Add at 20–40%; strongest on bright edges against dark.
- **Bloom**: threshold > ~0.7, blur 4–10% W, tint warm white `#FFF0E0`, Screen at 15–30%, slightly lifts
  shadows; pIr64 glow has bluish tint `#C0D0FF`.
- **Softness**: 0.5–1 px blur on the picture (sjgc) before grain.

### J. Colour / tone of old film (refs 4 + 2 implied: 9PSP, pIr64, sjgc, og3E; V8kx/s4Vq tints)
- **B&W silver** (9PSP): desaturate, contrast +20–30%, blacks crushed to `#0A0A0A`, whites `#E8E8E8`,
  vignette 20–30% at corners.
- **Faded colour stock** (pIr64, sjgc): lift blacks to `#1A1612`–`#22201C`, highlights capped
  `#F0EAD8`, saturation −20–35%, shadows teal `#203A3A`, highlights warm/cream `#F4E4C0`; channel mix
  slight green-cyan cast (Texturelabs).
- **Warm Super 8 / Kodachrome** (sjgc): skin orange, saturation −10%, warm highlight tint `#FFE0B0`.
- Process via Hue/Sat + Curves + Exposure + Levels + Vignette (og3E, pIr64).

### K. Other
- **Double-exposure flash transition** (og3E): two shots superimposed (Screen) + 0.3 s white peak.
- **Hand-scratched glyphs on emulsion** (ZedI): stroked squiggles 2–4% H, chalk white/ochre `#B8A060`,
  random every 2 frames — "experimental film" style, optional.
- **Edge code / edge print text** (3 refs: sjgc, V8kx, dMIn): small monospace numbers/letters in the
  film margin, bluish (`#5060A0`) or amber, sometimes ghosted into burns.

## Part 2b — the other families (no reference pack: spec from the studio author's knowledge)

Written for Film Look Studio Pro. Timings follow the house rule: an animated move must be
essentially finished by p≈0.95 of the layer (never dead by 0.7). Sizes are % of a 1080p frame
(S = short side/1080 like the engine). All amounts are seeded and deterministic.

### L. Film & damage (the texture family; grain/halation already specced in D/I/J)
1. **Grain** (process): per-frame monochrome noise, pixel size 1–2 px at 1080 (0.1–0.2% S),
   luminance-only by default; chroma grain optional (±3% saturation wobble). Density: σ 4–8%
   subtle, 10–16% heavy 16 mm, 20% "damaged". 8 mm look = hold the same noise field for
   2 frames at 24/30 fps (param `filmFps` 8–18). Grain sits in the mids: scale noise by
   4·l·(1−l) (l = pixel luma) so blacks/whites stay clean. Render at 1/2 resolution and
   upscale (soft grain, 4× faster).
2. **Dust & hair** (overlay, multiply for dark / screen for white): dark specks 0.1–0.6% S,
   0–10 per frame, 1-frame life (2 for 8 mm); white "negative dirt" sparkle variant. Hairs:
   bezier squiggle 0.2% S thick, 8–18% W long, dark, lives 1–4 frames, ≤1 per 2 s.
   Stains: soft dark ellipses 5–15% H at 10–25% opacity, live 0.5–2 s (few per layer).
3. **Scratches** (overlay): 1–3 vertical lines, width 0.25–0.5% W, full height; white
   (emulsion, screen blend) or dark (base, multiply); x wanders ±1% W on ~1 s smooth noise;
   opacity flickers 30–80%/frame; lines pop in/out every 0.5–2 s. Typical x 30–70% W.
4. **Gates** (overlay, multiply — black surround, picture window; drawn UNDER nothing, over
   everything): formats — 8 mm: window 67% W × 89% H centred, corner r 4% W; Super-8: window
   71% × 93%, one sprocket 12.5% W × 28% H at x 4.5–17%, thin warm outline; 16 mm: window
   78% × 92%, 2 sprocket columns at 6% and 94% W, 4-perf pitch 27% H, edge-print text amber
   `#E0A040` at 2.6% cap height; 35 mm: window 84% × 76% (letterboxed feel) with 4-perf
   columns both sides; "rough gate": custom window 93.8% × 75.9%, edge displaced by
   fractal ±0.4% W boiling at 2 Hz, inner broken line inset 3%, feather 2–3% W.
   Window tint warm grey `#DCD8D4` with +8% hotspot at centre; surround `#141410`–`#08080F`
   with light-spill halo 3% W hugging the window. Reel-change cue dots: black discs 5–7% H
   at ~85% W, 12% H, 4–8 frames.
5. **Gate weave / frame jitter** (camera): whole picture offset, smooth fbm 1–3 Hz,
   ±0.2–0.5% W horizontal, ±0.3–0.8% H vertical, rot ±0.1°; jumpy variant = new offset
   every film frame (registration error). ALWAYS return mx/my margins = worst-case amplitude
   so the engine's cover zoom hides the edge; never show canvas through a move.
6. **Frame slip** (camera, event 0.5–0.9 s): once per layer (seeded pick), the frame drops:
   picture jumps up/down by 20–35% H over 2–3 frames, hangs 2–4 frames, snaps back;
   engine `wrapY` draws the next frame's picture above/below separated by a 6% H black
   frame line. Feels like the projector losing sprockets.
7. **Exposure flicker** (process): per 1–2 film frames, brightness ×0.94–1.06 subtle /
   ×0.85–1.15 heavy; smooth over 1 frame. Optional colour wobble ±2% temp.

### M. Archival (old-photo look family)
1. **Sepia** (grade): mono toned: sat→0, toneDark `#241a10` → toneLight `#f0e2c8`, warm
   temp +0.35, faded 0.25, lift 0.03, contrast +0.15. Deep sepia = darker tone, more fade.
2. **Faded colour** (grade): lifted blacks `#1A1612`–`#22201C`, highlights capped `#F0EAD8`,
   sat 0.65, teal shadows `#203A3A` (split-tone shadow `#2E6A5E` at 0.3), cream highlights
   `#F4E4C0` at 0.25, slight green-cyan cast. "Washed-out memory" = more fade 0.45, sat 0.5.
3. **Silver B&W** (grade): sat 0, contrast +0.3, crush 0.35, toneDark `#0A0A0A`,
   toneLight `#E8E8E8`; harsh newsprint = contrast +0.45 + bleach 0.35 (silvery).
4. **Halftone** (process): newspaper print — dot screen at 45° (mono) or CMY at
   15/75/0/45°; cell 3–8 px at 1080; amount 0.5–1; keep picture readable at amount 0.6.
   Coarse screen print = cell 10–14 px, mono, high contrast.
5. **Old photo print** (process): yellowed paper tint `#E8D9B0` at 15–30% mix, slight soft
   focus (blur 1–2 px at 1080), foxing: 6–14 seeded brown blotches `#8a6a3a` 1–4% S at
   10–20% opacity, faded edges (vignette 0.3), gentle contrast loss. 1940s print = warm
   sepia + heavy foxing; 1970s = orange cast `#e0a070` + cyan shadows; album = fade 0.5.

### N. VHS / CRT / glitch
1. **VHS** (process): chroma bleed — copy red ±3–8 px horizontally into neighbours with
   0.35 alpha; horizontal jitter — 6–10 bands of 8–30 px each shifted ±2–6 px on their own
   smooth noise (new offset per film frame); bottom tracking bar: last 8–14% H = noisy
   grey smear with white tear lines, optional; slight saturation boost 1.15 and contrast
   −0.05 overall. SP/EP feel via param `wear` 0–1 scaling all of the above.
2. **CRT** (overlay): scanlines 2–3 px pitch at 1080 at 18–30% black; aperture grille:
   faint vertical RGB stripes 1–4% strength at cell ≥3 px; corner vignette 0.25–0.4;
   glass reflection: one soft diagonal white streak 10% W at 8% alpha top-left; optional
   barrel feel from a subtle radial scale (picture only, ±2%).
3. **RGB split** (process): shift R left/up and B right/down by 2–14 px (1080 scale),
   alpha-blend the shifted channels over the picture; fringe reads on high-contrast edges.
   Subtle = 2–3 px (stereo-film look), heavy = 10–14 px + 10% ghost double at 0.2 alpha.
4. **Glitch** (process): horizontal slice displacement — 4–14 slices of 8–60 px, each
   shifted ±10–80 px, slices re-picked every 2–4 film frames; RGB tear on the 2–3
   strongest slices (split 6–20 px); block noise: 8–24 rectangles 10–80 px of pure
   colour `#18f0ff`/`#ff2050`/white at 0.25 alpha; occasional 1-frame invert (2% chance
   per film frame). "Broadcast error" = sustained; "burst" = 2–3 bursts per layer of
   4–10 frames each (seeded schedule).
5. **Scanlines / interference** (overlay): fine lines 2 px at 12–18% + one rolling
   brighter/darker band 8–12% H travelling downward over 6–12 s (tv synch roll).

### O. Camera moves (all kind:'camera'; return margins mx/my = worst-case so no edge shows)
1. **Impact shake** (event 0.4–0.8 s): decaying vibration, freq 8–14 Hz, amplitude 0.4–1.2%
   of H decaying with exp(−t·5); direction param (all/vertical/horizontal); tiny rot ±0.3°.
   Explosion = 1.2%, slap = 0.5%, rumble = continuous 0.25% at 5 Hz (no decay).
2. **Handheld drift** (continuous): fbm 2 layers — slow float ±1.5–3% W/H over 6–10 s +
   micro jitter ±0.2% at 4–6 Hz; breathing sine ±0.4% at 0.25 Hz vertical. Documentary
   = calm 1.5%, nervous = 3% + 8 Hz micro, calm float = slow only.
3. **Zoom punch** (event 0.5–0.9 s): zoom 1 → 1.18–1.30 in 0.12–0.2 s (ease-out),
   settle back to 1.04–1.08 by p≈0.9 (never below 1.0!); 4–8% zoom-blur during the
   punch (engine zb); optional slight counter-move x/y. Double punch = hit, settle,
   hit again (schedule at 0.3/0.7).
4. **Whip pan** (event 0.35–0.55 s): picture travels 55–90% W left or right with strong
   motion blur (engine bx 30–60 px at 1080 scale) mid-move, easing in-out; ends parked
   at ±2% off-centre. Reads as a fast pan over a cut.
5. **Slow float (Ken Burns)** (continuous): zoom drifts 1 → 1.06–1.12 across the whole
   layer (linear-ish, reaches full target only at p=1), pan ±2–4% on a fixed diagonal;
   archival-stills feel. Never zoom < 1.

### P. Frame & grade
1. **Vignette** (overlay): radial falloff to black (or warm `#2a1408`) — corner strength
   0.25–0.5, start of falloff 55–75% of half-diagonal, smooth cosine; oval (projector)
   or rectangular (gate) shape param. Heavy projector oval = 0.65 with early falloff 40%.
2. **Letterbox** (overlay): hard black bars top+bottom; ratios 2.35:1 (bars 10.6% H
   each), 2:1 (12.5%), 1.85:1 (7.3%), 1.66:1 (5.1%); optional 1 s slide-in from 0.
   Cinema feel = 2.35 + vignette 0.25.
3. **Grade looks** (grade kind, exportable as .cube):
   - **Teal & orange**: split-tone shadows `#2E6A5E` 0.35, highlights `#FFB36B` 0.3,
     sat 1.05, contrast +0.12, pivot 0.42 — the modern documentary film look.
   - **War documentary**: sat 0.45, contrast +0.2, crush 0.3, temp −0.1, teal-grey
     shadows `#3A4A4A` 0.25, redBoost 0.35 (keeps blood/fire warm), fade 0.15.
   - **Warm history**: temp +0.3, golden highlights `#FFD9A0` 0.28, sat 0.85, lift 0.04,
     soft contrast +0.08 — nostalgia / heritage stories.
   - **Bleach bypass**: bleach 0.55, contrast +0.3, sat 0.55 — silvery harsh archive.
   - **Noir interview**: sat 0.1, contrast +0.28, crush 0.4, toneLight `#EDEDE8`.
   Each look as its own effect id with an `amount` (strength) param, a couple of
   fine-tune params (temperature, strength of the split), and 1–2 named presets.

### Q. Timing rules (all families)
- One-shot moves/events: finish by p≈0.95, hold (not disappear) after; event:true +
   def.dur so presets get a length.
- Continuous textures (grain, dust, vhs): no beginning/end, register WITHOUT event/dur,
   default dur null = whole layer; the p envelope must not modulate them (they are
   stateless at any t).
- Never modulate a continuous texture by p; DO modulate one-shot events by p.
