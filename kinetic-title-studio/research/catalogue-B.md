# Catalogue B - text/graphic animation effects from 14 YouTube videos

Method: every contact sheet of every video was viewed (6x6 tiles, 200 px, timestamped). Tiles are small, so shapes/positions are read at thumbnail level; timings are estimated from tile timestamps (0.25-0.6 s spacing in the first two minutes of each video, then ~8 s - only useful for "does it exist", not for motion). Where a sheet is mostly software UI it is said so. "Watchable" = does the video contain finished, legible results worth copying.

Screen-position vocabulary: frame is 16:9; "lower-left" = bottom-left safe area, etc.
