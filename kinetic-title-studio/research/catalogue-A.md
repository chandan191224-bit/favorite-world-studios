# Catalogue A - text-animation effects from YouTube contact sheets

Source: 6x6 contact sheets (200 px tiles, yellow timestamps). Videos covered: 14. All sheets of every video were viewed.
Timing numbers are estimates read from tile timestamps (sheet resolution is 0.25-0.5 s inside the first 120 s, then ~8-10 s per tile, so long-tutorial results are only seen in the first two minutes).
Conventions: "hold" = fully readable time; "in/out" = approximate animation duration.

## Most important numbers for Kinetic Title Studio
- Typical title card hold: 0.75-2.5 s; entrance 0.0-0.5 s; exit 0.25-0.5 s (often a fade or the next hard cut).
- Word cadence for kinetic captions: 0.25-0.5 s per word; stagger within a phrase 0.1-0.25 s; blur trail 2-3 frames; overshoot pop 0.7 -> 1.1 -> 1.0.
- Match cut: ~0.5 s per shot, 6-8 shots, text height equal across shots, highlight rectangle on target word, hold the last 1.5-2 s.
- Hormozi-style caption: 1-3 words, white caps with black stroke, active word yellow/green/red.
- Wide-tracked letter reveal (THE HONOR): letters appear one by one every ~0.25 s over a 1 s span.
- Typewriter: ~10-12 chars/s, mono font, ink roughness + paper texture + slow camera push.
- Devanagari: stroke-on write-on within 0.5 s then a long static hold with minimal drift; keep a chroma-green variant.
